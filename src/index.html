About me

Genoa, Italy

Making things has always been my favourite way to spend time. Drawing came first, and it never stopped: characters, worlds, and the stories that hold them together. Give me a blank page and I'll come back with someone who has a past, a reason to fight and a costume that tells both.

At Event Horizon School that habit became a craft: three years of concept art, learning to design with a reason behind every line, to work in a team, and to trust a process from the first moodboard to the final render. That's where drawing turned into design.

Then came code, because I wanted to see the things I design actually come to life. The same curiosity that once made me build my own PC, just to see what was inside, took me from interfaces on paper to interfaces that work. Now art and code feed each other: an illustrator's eye for the screen, a programmer's logic for the worlds I write.

Every project starts the same way, with an idea I can't stop thinking about. The passion for turning it into something real, a character, an interface or a game, is the part of this work I love most.
